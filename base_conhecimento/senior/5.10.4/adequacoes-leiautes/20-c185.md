| Número | Campo | Preenchimento |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 06 | COD\_MOT\_REST\_COMPL | E660RSV.CtaB57 |
| 11 | VL\_UNIT\_ICMS\_OP\_CONV | E660RSM.MedIcm |
| 12 | VL\_UNIT\_BC\_ICMS\_ST\_ESTOQUE\_CONV | E660RSM.MedIcm |
| 13 | VL\_UNIT\_ICMS\_ST\_ESTOQUE\_CONV | E660RSM.MedIcs + E660RSM.MedFcp |
| 14 | VL\_UNIT\_FCP\_ICMS\_ST\_ESTOQUE\_CONV | E660RSM.MedFcp |
