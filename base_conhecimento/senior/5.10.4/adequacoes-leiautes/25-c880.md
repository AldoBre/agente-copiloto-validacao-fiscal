A partir do leiaute 15 e data inicial **01/01/2021**, há a ligação com a tabela E660RSM para retornar os campos **E660RSM.MedIcm, E660RSM.MedIcs e E660RSM.MedFcp**, que irão preencher os valores das médias.

| Número | Campo | Preenchimento |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | VL\_UNIT\_ICMS\_OP\_CONV | E660RSM.MedIcm |
| 8 | VL\_UNIT\_BC\_ICMS\_ST\_ESTOQUE\_CONV | E660RSM.MedIcm |
| 9 | VL\_UNIT\_ICMS\_ST\_ESTOQUE\_CONV | E660RSM.MedIcs + E660RSM.MedFcp |
| 10 | VL\_UNIT\_FCP\_ICMS\_ST\_ESTOQUE\_CONV | E660RSM.MedFcp |
